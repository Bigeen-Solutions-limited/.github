# Security Policy

If you find a security issue in any Bigeen Solutions repository, product (StitchFYN, Talent Governance System, Operis, Bigeen Core) or website, please tell us privately.

**Do not open a public issue.**

## How to report

Email **[SECURITY-EMAIL]** with:

- What you found and where (repo, URL or product)
- Steps to reproduce
- What an attacker could do with it

## What happens next

- We acknowledge your report within 3 working days.
- We keep you updated while we fix it.
- We credit you once it's resolved, if you'd like that.

Please give us reasonable time to fix the issue before you disclose it, and don't access or change data that isn't yours.

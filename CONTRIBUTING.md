# Contributing to Bigeen Solutions

Thanks for taking the time. Most of our repositories are maintained by the Bigeen team, but we welcome bug reports and suggestions.

## Reporting a bug or suggesting a change

1. Check the open issues first. Someone may have raised it already.
2. Open a new issue using the right template.
3. Be specific: what you expected, what happened and how to reproduce it.

## Pull requests

- Open an issue before starting on anything large, so we can agree on the approach.
- Keep each pull request focused on one change.
- Describe what changed and why, and how you tested it.
- Never commit secrets, credentials, client data or personal data.

## Ground rules

- Client work is confidential and never goes in a public repository.
- Personal data is handled in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA): collect the minimum, and never expose or share it.
- Everyone follows our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

# Code of Conduct

Bigeen builds systems that work for people. That means we treat people well in our own spaces too.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and professional, even when you disagree.
- Give feedback that is specific and useful, and receive it the same way.
- Respect people's time, privacy and confidentiality.
- Assume good intent and ask before you judge.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Sharing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Personal attacks or deliberately disrupting discussions.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **[CONDUCT-EMAIL]**. Reports are handled in confidence by the founders. We may warn, temporarily restrict, or remove anyone who breaks this code from our repositories and spaces.

This code applies in all Bigeen Solutions repositories, issues, pull requests and community spaces.
